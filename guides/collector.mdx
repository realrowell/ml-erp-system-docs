The **Collector** role is for field staff who visit vendos to collect cash and record transactions. After you sign in, the app takes you to the collector dashboard at `/collector/dashboard`. You can only see and act on the screens described in this guide.

## Who should use this guide

Use this guide if you:

- Belong to a collection team
- Visit customer sites to pick up cash from vendos
- Record collections against customer-vendo contracts

## The collector dashboard

The dashboard is built around the **current collection period**. When you open it, the app finds the active period and shows you two lists:

- **Due today** — contracts assigned to your collection team for the current period that have not been collected yet. Work this list top to bottom.
- **All contracts in this period** — every contract scheduled for the period, with a flag showing whether collection has already been recorded. Use it to confirm your progress and check on units you have already visited.

If no collection period is active, the dashboard is empty. Contact an admin to confirm the schedule.

### What each contract card shows

Each card on the dashboard is a customer-vendo contract. It includes:

- The customer's name and address (barangay and municipality)
- The vendo assigned to that contract
- Whether the contract has been collected for the current period

Tap a card to drill into the contract and start the collection.

## Collecting from a vendo (QR scanner)

The fastest way to record a collection in the field is the QR scanner. Open **QR scanner** from the side navigation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Point your device camera at the QR sticker on the vendo. The app looks up the unit, confirms its customer and contract, and opens the collection flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount collected">
    Record the cash you are taking from the unit. The amount is recorded as a `collection` transaction with the source set to `mobile`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Submit the transaction. The contract on your dashboard immediately flips to "collected" for the current period, and the entry shows up in FinOps for admins to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transactions you create

Collections you record from the app are saved as transactions. Each one carries:

| Field    | Value you'll see                                                  |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Type     | `collection`                                                       |
| Status   | `pending` until an admin confirms, then `confirmed`                |
| Source   | `mobile` (created from the collector app)                          |

If you make a mistake, do not try to delete the entry. Flag it to an admin so they can record an `adjustment` or `refund` transaction against the same contract.

## Tips for a smooth route

- Sort your "Due today" list by location so you visit nearby customers together.
- Scan the QR sticker rather than searching by hand — it pulls the right customer and contract automatically.
- Record each collection before moving to the next site, while the cash count is fresh.
- If a vendo looks damaged or offline, tell your supervisor so a technician can open a repair ticket.
